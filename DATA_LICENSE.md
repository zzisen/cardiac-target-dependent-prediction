# Data and documentation license

Project-authored derived tables, figure source data, figures and documentation
are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Credit the authors and manuscript, cite the upstream sources and indicate changes.

The license does not replace upstream rights. Canonical exports of the Awinda,
regulatory-light-chain inhibitor, Tanner and human atrial source data retain
CC BY 4.0. Radbill source observations retain CC0 1.0. Published rat and human
model inputs retain Apache 2.0; copies of the original notices accompany them.

The V3 cytometry source record (Dryad pvmcvdnxc, version 4) retains CC0 1.0. Project-authored aggregated channel fractions, predictions, and derived summaries use CC BY 4.0 with source attribution. Raw FCS files and the large upstream ZIP are omitted. The small RLC workbook addition retains upstream CC BY 4.0 rather than being relicensed as project-authored material.
